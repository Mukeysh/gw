02 / The growth system

One journey. Five connected decisions.

01 / Capture

Know the signal.

Collect meaningful first-party interactions without turning the experience into a form farm.

02 / Context

Know the person.

Connect consented identity, account, audience and behavioural context where appropriate.

03 / Intent

Know the moment.

Translate interactions into useful intent signals, qualification rules and audience states.

04 / Activation

Make the next move.

Trigger content, campaigns, notifications or sales workflows through the right systems.

05 / Learning

Close the loop.

Feed outcomes back into analytics so campaigns improve instead of simply multiplying.

04 / Enterprise guardrails

The fastest campaign is useless if nobody can defend how it works.

Consent by default

Design identity and activation around explicit data boundaries, consent state and regional requirements.

Human escalation

Keep commercial, editorial and high-impact decisions reviewable instead of hiding them inside opaque automation.

Traceable outcomes

Every important trigger should be observable: what happened, why it fired and what changed afterward.
