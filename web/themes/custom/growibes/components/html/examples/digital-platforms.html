Growth becomes expensive when every market rebuilds the same thing.

Enterprise organizations accumulate sites, brands, languages, teams and content operations over years. The platform challenge is not simply “build Drupal.” It is deciding what should be shared, what should be local, and how change moves safely across the network.

Its current CMS strategy also explicitly highlights localization, enterprise integrations, content modeling, governance, accessibility and long-term maintainability.
