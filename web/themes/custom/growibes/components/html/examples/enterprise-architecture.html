01 / Start with the economics of change

The architecture question is not
“Can it scale?”

It is “What happens to the business when it does?”

A platform that supports ten times the traffic but requires ten times the teams, ten times the manual coordination and ten times the architectural exceptions has not truly scaled.

01 / AUDIENCE

Reach more people.

Brands, markets, languages, channels and journeys can expand without creating a new foundation for every audience.

02 / REVENUE

Monetize more capability.

New products, services and digital journeys can build on reusable platform, data and integration capability.

03 / VELOCITY

Move faster.

Teams can ship meaningful change without every release becoming an architectural negotiation.

04 / LEVERAGE

Make complexity cheaper.

Successful patterns become enterprise capabilities instead of one-off projects that must be reinvented.

One foundation.
More ways to create value.

01 / BUILD ONCE

Reusable capability

Shared content, identity, APIs, design systems, data models and platform services become building blocks.

02 / EXTEND

New business surface

03 / COMPOUND

Lower marginal change

Every proven pattern can make the next launch faster, safer and more predictable.

04 / The decisions that determine scale

Good architecture makes
the trade-offs visible.

01 / MARKET

Standardize ↔ localize

Share what creates leverage. Differentiate where the market demands it.

02 / PRODUCT

Platform ↔ project

Promote repeatable capability into infrastructure instead of rebuilding it.

03 / DATA

Centralize ↔ federate

Put truth where it creates the most leverage without creating organizational drag.

04 / AI

Automate ↔ govern

Give intelligence room to act while keeping important boundaries explicit.

05 / INVESTMENT

Build ↔ buy

Own what differentiates the business. Buy what does not need to.
