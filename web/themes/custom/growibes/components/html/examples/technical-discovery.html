02 / The free 14-day sprint

Two weeks.
On us.

Start with a real backlog ticket, architecture question or delivery problem. The first 14 days are free. The objective is not to manufacture a giant strategy document. It is to create enough evidence for the next decision.

DAYS 01–02
Frame the problem.

Stakeholders, constraints, existing architecture, success conditions and the decision that needs to be made.

DAYS 03–05
Inspect the system.

Code, content, integrations, delivery model and technical dependencies are examined at the depth the problem requires.

DAYS 06–09
Test the hard part.

Prototype, spike, architecture experiment or production-oriented implementation — whichever produces the most useful evidence.

DAYS 10–12
Pressure-test the path.

Risks, trade-offs, security, performance, maintainability and delivery implications are made explicit.

DAYS 13–14
Decide what happens next.

Evidence, recommendations and an executable next step — continue, change direction or stop.
