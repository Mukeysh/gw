01 · The integration problem

More systems.
More seams.

Enterprise platforms rarely fail because a single system is incapable. They become difficult when every new capability creates another integration, another data path, another dependency and another team that has to understand how everything fits together.

The modern direction is toward API-driven, cloud-native and composable architectures — but the architecture still needs governance, ownership and a clear operating model.
02 · The connected stack

We focus on the boundaries between systems — because that is where complexity, reliability risk and hidden operational cost tend to accumulate.
