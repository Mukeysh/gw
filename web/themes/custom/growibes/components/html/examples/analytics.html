01 / The problem

Dashboards don't create insight. Decisions do.

Enterprise teams rarely suffer from a shortage of analytics. They suffer from disconnected analytics: one system for traffic, another for campaigns, another for content, another for product behavior — and no shared model of what matters.

Drupal's 2026 product strategy explicitly includes content analytics alongside personalization, SEO/AEO, lead generation and privacy compliance. The opportunity is to make measurement part of the digital platform rather than a reporting layer bolted onto it.

04 / Optimization

Make improvement a system, not a quarterly project.

Optimization works when measurement closes the loop: observe the behavior, form a hypothesis, change the experience, test the result, learn and repeat.

That approach is already visible in current enterprise digital-experience practice: Acquia describes experimentation as valuable even when a test does not produce a lift, because it prevents teams from scaling ineffective approaches.

05 / AI-ready analytics

Give AI better questions — and better evidence.

AI can help summarize patterns, identify anomalies, classify feedback and surface optimization opportunities. But the quality of the answer depends on the quality and governance of the underlying data.

Drupal's current AI direction includes data-and-insights agents and optimization agents, alongside governance and enterprise integration. That makes a clean measurement foundation increasingly valuable.
