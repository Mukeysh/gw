04 / Governance

Give AI enough freedom to be useful — and enough boundaries to be trusted.

Permission-aware

AI should respect the same content access and business boundaries that humans and applications already operate within.

Human-in-the-loop

High-impact content and operational actions can move through review, approval and escalation rather than disappearing into automation.

Observable

Track prompts, retrieval, model decisions, actions and outcomes where appropriate so teams can diagnose and improve the system.

06 / Why Drupal is interesting now

Drupal is becoming the governed backend AI can actually work with.

Drupal's 2026 AI roadmap explicitly focuses on page generation, context management, background agents, design-system integration, content discovery, advanced governance, intelligent website improvements and multi-channel campaigns. Its AI initiative is also developing “Inside AI” for people using Drupal and “Outside AI” for external agents acting on Drupal.
