03 / Architecture with consequences

We don't draw the future state. We make the trade-offs visible.

Every recommendation must survive contact with the real organization.

That means accounting for content operations, legacy dependencies, security, procurement, internal capability, migration effort, integration complexity and the pace at which the business can actually absorb change.

Drupal's current core strategy explicitly positions Core as a platform for customized CMS solutions, from marketer-friendly Drupal CMS through high-code and headless architectures. That flexibility makes architecture decisions — not platform slogans — the strategic work.

04 / Transformation without theater

A roadmap should tell leadership what to do on Monday morning.

Executive clarity

A concise transformation thesis, decision log, business case and north-star outcomes that leadership can defend.

Engineering clarity

Target architecture, capability map, technical principles and an ordered backlog that teams can actually execute.

Operational clarity

Ownership, governance, dependencies, migration waves and measures that keep the transformation moving after strategy ends.

07 / Why now

The platform is changing faster than the transformation cycle.

Drupal's 2026 strategy now explicitly frames the platform around enterprise readiness, AI-enabled workflows, governance, structured content, localization, scalability and long-term maintainability. Drupal 11.4 also introduced major performance and developer-experience improvements.
