01 / The problem

Commerce breaks when content, customer and transaction live in different worlds.

Enterprise commerce is rarely just a cart and checkout. B2B pricing, subscriptions, inventory, tax, customer accounts, promotions, product information and editorial content all have to cooperate.

Drupal Commerce is deliberately framework-first: it supports custom product types, variations, order types, checkout flows, promotions, payments, taxes and extensibility. Drupal can also act as the experience layer around an external commerce engine.

03 / Where enterprise commerce gets hard

The edge cases are the business.

Simple storefronts are easy to demo. Enterprise commerce is defined by the rules underneath them.

B2B

Different customers. Different economics.

Account pricing, customer groups, purchasing workflows and repeat-order journeys need to coexist with the public experience.

GLOBAL

Many markets. One operating model.

Support currencies, languages, tax rules, local payment methods and market-specific catalog or availability logic.

CONTENT

Product pages should sell.

Connect product information with editorial stories, education, comparison and discovery instead of isolating commerce from content.

05 / Architecture

Use Drupal where it creates advantage.

DRUPAL COMMERCE

Commerce-native architecture

Use Drupal Commerce when product, order and transaction logic belongs inside the Drupal platform.

EXPERIENCE LAYER

Drupal + external commerce

Keep transactional systems specialized while Drupal owns content-rich product experiences and presentation.

INTEGRATION

PIM / ERP / CRM / OMS

Connect the systems that own products, inventory, customers, finance and fulfillment instead of duplicating truth.

HEADLESS

APIs across every channel

Expose commerce capabilities to web, mobile, applications and other digital touchpoints when the experience requires it.

06 / AI + commerce

Make discovery smarter.
Keep transactions governed.

AI can improve product discovery, recommendations, customer support and content operations. But the transaction system still needs deterministic rules around price, eligibility, inventory, tax and payment.

Drupal's current commerce direction emphasizes content-rich shopping, personalization, product discovery and multi-market scale, while its commerce ecosystem continues to support deep integrations.
