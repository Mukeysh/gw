01 / The post-launch problem

Launch day is not the finish line. It is the beginning of the real platform.

Enterprise platforms accumulate dependencies, content, integrations, traffic patterns and business expectations. Without senior continuity, small changes become risky, technical debt compounds and every new initiative starts by rediscovering how the system works.

04 / The outcome

The real product is confidence to change.

Less rediscovery

Architecture knowledge stays with the team instead of disappearing into tickets, documents or contractor handoffs.

Faster decisions

Small improvements and urgent fixes can move through an established engineering system instead of restarting a project.

Better compounding

Every release can improve the platform's standards, observability and architecture rather than adding another layer of complexity.

05 / Why this matters now

The platform is becoming an operating system for AI.

Drupal's 2026 strategy increasingly treats AI as infrastructure across workflows, while the AI Initiative is developing both “Inside AI” capabilities for people using Drupal and “Outside AI” capabilities for agents acting on Drupal. That makes continuous engineering more important: the platform needs to remain observable, governed, secure and adaptable as new AI workflows arrive.
