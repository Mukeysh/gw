Swiss watchmakers found the same principle in their own quiet valleys, four hundred years of undistracted attention turned into precision the world still trusts.

Technology can create high-value work with a lighter physical footprint, giving Himalayan communities another path to prosperity — without every new opportunity demanding more concrete, construction or pressure on the landscape.
