01 / The agency problem

You win the work. Then the engineering problem starts.

The hard part isn't always finding demand. It is having enough senior technical capacity when the right project arrives — without hiring ahead of revenue, handing delivery to an unreliable freelancer network, or turning away work your agency should have won.
